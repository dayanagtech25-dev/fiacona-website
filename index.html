Who We Are

FIACONA is a Washington, D.C.-based voluntary, charitable organization that advocates on behalf of the one million-strong Indian American Christians from all 50 states and Canada. It is a coalition of Protestant, Catholic, Orthodox, Evangelical, Pentecostal, and independent church and civic organizations.

FIACONA serves as a bridge builder for ecumenical advocacy, focusing on reducing violence against Christians in India.

What We Do

Support

Upholding persecuted Christian communities in India through prayer, solidarity and relief awareness.

Advocacy

Engaging U.S. lawmakers, government officials and civil society to raise awareness of human rights issues.

Documentation

Monitoring and reporting incidents of violence and legislative restrictions against Christians in India.
