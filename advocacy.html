Advocacy

Defending religious freedom and human rights

Raising Our Voice

FIACONA engages with members of Congress, the White House, the State Department and international bodies, presenting documented cases of religious freedom violations in India and urging U.S. policy that upholds human rights.

Persecution Reports

Each year FIACONA monitors and reports on persecution in the form of physical violence, legislative assaults, and socio-economic and political restrictions directed toward over 100 million Indians who identify as Christians. In 2021 alone, more than 580 incidents were reported, ranging from neighborhood skirmishes to lynching and armed assaults. Many attacks go undocumented, especially in rural areas.

Country of Particular Concern (CPC)

The U.S. Commission on International Religious Freedom (USCIRF) has repeatedly recommended that India be designated a Country of Particular Concern. FIACONA supports this recommendation, which would require the U.S. State Department to address religious freedom violations in India.

Congressional Engagement

FIACONA's Advocacy Director meets with U.S. lawmakers and submits memoranda to senior White House advisors on religious freedom, and hosts forums and webinars on the challenges facing Christians in India today.

Legislative Fellowship

FIACONA's annual Fellowship Program places Congressional fellows who are fully Congressional staff, supported, paid and advised through FIACONA, in full-time positions in Washington, D.C. Responsibilities include event planning (such as Congressional briefings), research, speech writing, drafting letters and legislation supporting religious freedom and human rights, and meetings with the State Department, foreign embassies and NGOs.

Applicants must be legal U.S. residents with a bachelor's degree (Master's preferred), strong writing skills, and relevant experience in grassroots work, international relations, research or journalism. Required: writing samples, two letters of recommendation, a resume and a completed application.
