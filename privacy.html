Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

FIACONA respects your privacy. We collect only the information you voluntarily provide (for example, when you contact us, sign up, or donate) and use it solely to respond to you and carry out our mission. We do not sell or rent personal information. Donation details are handled by the payment provider you choose (bank, Zelle, check or PayPal). For questions, contact info@fiacona.org.

FIACONA is a Washington, D.C.-based coalition advocating for the religious freedom and safety of Christians in India.
