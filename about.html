About Us

A coalition of Indian American churches and organizations

Our Story

FIACONA is a Washington, D.C.-based voluntary, charitable organization that advocates on behalf of the one million-strong Indian American Christians from all 50 states and Canada. It is a coalition of Protestant, Catholic, Orthodox, Evangelical, Pentecostal, and independent church and civic organizations, primarily comprising Indian Americans.

FIACONA serves as a bridge builder for ecumenical advocacy, focusing on reducing violence against Christians in India.

Our Mission

Our mission is to preserve the socio-political space of the Christian church from encroachment by nationalist radical groups acting in the name of Hinduism, as well as their representatives in the Indian government. Protecting the interest of the Christian church in the Union of India is the primary mission of FIACONA.

Ensuring the social, cultural and political space of the church, besides the protection of fundamental rights to safety, security and equal status of every single person following the Christian faith in the Union of India, remains a priority for FIACONA.

The threat to lives and properties of Christian communities in India, in spite of having all imaginable protections enshrined in the Constitution of India, remains a serious concern for the Federation.

Our Focus

Pray

Upholding persecuted Christian communities in India through prayer and solidarity.

Engage

Meeting with U.S. lawmakers, officials and civil society to raise awareness of religious freedom violations.

Unite

Building ecumenical partnerships across denominations and organizations in North America.

FIACONA is a Washington, D.C.-based coalition advocating for the religious freedom and safety of Christians in India.
